Step 2 · Win

The goal

Checkmate the enemy general. No legal move means you lose, not draw.

Check means a general is under attack. You must answer it immediately, in one of three ways:

  • Move the general to a safe point,
  • Block the line of attack, or
  • Capture the attacking piece.

If none of those works, it is checkmate. Before you capture anything, ask: is it protected? If an enemy piece can take back, you have made a trade, not won material.

Step 5 · Avoid surprises

Three rules that surprise newcomers

帥
Generals cannot face each other

If the generals face each other down an open file with nothing between them, the position is illegal. You also cannot move a piece off that file if it would open it.

傌
Horses and elephants can be blocked

A horse needs its leg (adjacent point) empty. An elephant needs its eye (middle of jump) empty.

炮
Cannons need a screen to capture

A cannon moves like a chariot but captures by jumping exactly one piece and landing on an enemy beyond.

Step 6 · Your first game

Your first game plan

  1. Central cannon.
    Slide a cannon in front of your general to pressure the central soldier.
  2. Develop chariots.
    Your strongest pieces start in corners. Open files for them, and don’t trade a chariot for a lesser piece.
  3. Mind the legs.
    Before every horse or elephant move, check it isn’t blocked—and look for ways to block your opponent’s.
