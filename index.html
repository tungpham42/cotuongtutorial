Step 3 · Win

The goal

Checkmate the enemy general. Or leave your opponent with no legal move. In Xiangqi, having no legal move means you lose, not draw.

Step 5 · Avoid surprises

Three rules that surprise newcomers

帥
Generals cannot face each other

If the two generals look at each other down an open file with nothing between them, that position is illegal. This can force a win, so watch the file.

傌
Horses and elephants can be blocked

A horse needs the point next to it (its “leg”) to be empty. An elephant needs the point in the middle of its jump (its “eye”) to be empty.

炮
Cannons need a screen to capture

A cannon moves like a chariot, but to capture it must hop over exactly one piece of either colour, then land on an enemy behind it.

Step 6 · Your first game

Your first game plan

  1. Open with the cannon to the centre.
    Slide a cannon in front of your general. It pressures the enemy’s central soldier right away.
  2. Get your chariots out.
    They are your strongest pieces and are stuck in the corners. Open a file for each one.
  3. Keep guards home, attack with the rest.
    Advisors and elephants rarely leave. Use chariots, horses, and cannons to chase the general.
